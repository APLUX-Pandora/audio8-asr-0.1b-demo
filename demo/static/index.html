Live transcription

Open an audio file to listen to it and choose Upload and transcribe to send its audio from the beginning. The browser must support the format. Audio uploads are limited to 64 MiB and 10 minutes.

For a local or built-in video, Start plays from the current position and sends its audio. Pause confirms received speech. Seeking during transcription clears the displayed transcript and starts at the new position.

Microphone sends sound while transcription is running. Use a secure page and allow microphone access. Stop ends capture.

Temporary text may change as more speech arrives. Timing values count audio duration. Continuous speech uses overlapping windows, so boundary words may repeat or change. Copy and export include confirmed text. Use one session at a time on this device.
